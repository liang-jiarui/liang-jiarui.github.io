Mourgela et al. 2026 · Wildfire radiative effects and climate response

Archive analysis · Wildfire · Radiative forcing · EC-Earth3

Replication report

Recompute radiative effects and climate diagnostics from archived EC-Earth3-AerChem output.

Replication evidence

The archive analysis compares aerosol–cloud and aerosol–radiation effects, spatial significance, regional attribution, and fast and slow climate responses. The ozone contribution remains part of the radiative residual until a dedicated radiation calculation is available.
