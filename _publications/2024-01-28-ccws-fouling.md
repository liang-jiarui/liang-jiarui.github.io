This paper proposes a mixed-integer nonlinear programming model for the long-term operation of circulating cooling water systems under fouling. The model considers pipeline fouling, the dynamic concentration cycle, and variable-frequency drives to optimize heat transfer, pressure drop, energy use, and water conservation together.
