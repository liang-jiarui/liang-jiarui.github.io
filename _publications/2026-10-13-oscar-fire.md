This preprint presents OSCAR-fire v1.0, a wildfire carbon dynamics module for the OSCAR compact Earth system model. It extends OSCAR's land carbon bookkeeping with coarse woody debris and pyrogenic carbon pools, so a fire produces instantaneous combustion emissions, legacy dead-carbon release, and gradual post-fire ecosystem recovery. The manuscript is open for discussion and under review for <i>Geoscientific Model Development</i>.
