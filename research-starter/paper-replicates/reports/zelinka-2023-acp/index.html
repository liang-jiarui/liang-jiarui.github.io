Zelinka et al. 2023 · Aerosol forcing with APRP

Archive analysis · Aerosols · Radiative forcing · CMIP

Replication report

Recompute aerosol effective radiative forcing with approximate partial radiative perturbation and compare it with aerosol-free radiation calls.

Replication evidence

The analysis compares archived APRP calculations and double radiation calls for CMIP5 and CMIP6 members. The detailed report tracks unavailable members, numerical agreement and radiative budget closure.
